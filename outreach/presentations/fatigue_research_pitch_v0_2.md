## Positioning

This deck does not claim that the current 1D pure-Al model is already a validated industrial blade-life predictor. It presents the current work as a falsifiable paper project:

- build a physics-based probabilistic state-evolution framework;
- replace reversible critical-tail interpretation with absorbing first-passage initiation probability;
- separate rate-dependent hysteresis work from irreversible initiation probability;
- define the missing experimental validation package;
- ask for expert review, fatigue-machine time, specimen/data support, and potential co-authorship.

## Best use

Use this deck for:

1. KAIST/GIST/POSTECH/SKKU fatigue, fracture, or materials labs;
2. KIMS or similar materials institutes;
3. companies such as Doosan Enerbility / Hanwha Aerospace after at least one expert review round.

## Presenter stance

Lead with: “We are not asking you to adopt a finished model. We want to test whether this model can survive expert review and minimal fatigue validation.”
