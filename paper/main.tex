\begin{abstract}
We derive the exact generalized Lennard--Jones energy of an infinite uniform
one-dimensional chain, distinguish it from the cross-gap energy of two half
chains, and formulate a controlled metastable spacing-density dynamics.
Finite-rate probability transport produces phase-lag hysteresis; irreversible
crack initiation requires probability escape. Results without a calibrated
representative area and mobility are dimensionless demonstrations, not an
aluminum fatigue-life prediction.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem definition and scope}
The active material theory describes pure single-crystal aluminum under scalar
normal tension using only spacing $a$ and density $P(a,t)$. FCC, shear, slip,
Rubin chains, multiaxial fatigue and crystal plasticity are inactive archives.
Two- or three-dimensional meshes may display geometry and scalar axial stress
but do not change the constitutive theory. Every run declares a cubic loading
direction $[hkl]$. If cubic constants are supplied, only the scalar directional
modulus is projected:
\begin{equation}
E_{[hkl]}^{-1}=S_{11}-2(S_{11}-S_{12}-S_{44}/2)
(l_1^2l_2^2+l_2^2l_3^2+l_3^2l_1^2).
\end{equation}
The appearance of $S_{44}$ in this anisotropic elastic projection does not
introduce shear loading or a shear-fatigue criterion.

\section{Finite-chain pair energy and bidirectional counting}
For
$v(r)=\varepsilon_{\rm LJ}[(\sigma_{\rm LJ}/r)^m-(\sigma_{\rm LJ}/r)^n]$,
$m>n>1$, separation $ka$ occurs $N-k$ times, hence
\begin{equation}U_N(a)=\sum_{1\le i<j\le N}v(|i-j|a)
=\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}(N-k)v(ka).\end{equation}
An interior atom has two directions, while the factor $1/2$ removes pair
duplication. The usual 12--6 convention $4\epsilon[(\sigma/r)^{12}-(\sigma/r)^6]$
is separate; only for that special form is $\varepsilon_{\rm LJ}=4\epsilon$.

\section{Exact Riemann-zeta thermodynamic limit}
Using $\zeta(s)=\sum_{k\ge1}k^{-s}$,
\begin{equation}
U_\infty=\lim_{N\to\infty}{U_N\over N}
={1\over2}\sum_{k\ne0}v(|k|a)=\sum_{k\ge1}v(ka)
=\varepsilon_{\rm LJ}\left[\zeta(m)(\sigma_{\rm LJ}/a)^m
-\zeta(n)(\sigma_{\rm LJ}/a)^n\right].
\end{equation}
This is energy per atom or representative cell, not total chain energy.

\section{Homogeneous spacing energy versus local crack-gap energy}
For half-chains of internal spacing $a_0$ separated by $g$, let $q=g/a_0$.
There are $\ell+1$ cross-gap pairs with $i+j=\ell$, and
\begin{align}
S_p(q)&=\sum_{\ell\ge0}(\ell+1)(q+\ell)^{-p}
=\zeta(p-1,q)+(1-q)\zeta(p,q),\\
U_{\rm gap}&=\varepsilon_{\rm LJ}\left[(\sigma_{\rm LJ}/a_0)^mS_m(q)
-(\sigma_{\rm LJ}/a_0)^nS_n(q)\right].
\end{align}
$U_\infty$ is exact for homogeneous dilation; $U_{\rm gap}$ is direct for one
formed local opening. The active pre-initiation coordinate is the representative
spacing of a small approximately affine atomic neighbourhood, so $U_\infty$ is
the active local-homogeneous energy. $U_{\rm gap}$ is retained only for a future
post-initiation model and the two are not mixed.

\section{Equilibrium and tangent instability}
Stationarity and zero tangent stiffness give
\begin{align}
a_0&=\sigma_{\rm LJ}\left[{m\zeta(m)\over n\zeta(n)}\right]^{1/(m-n)},\\
a_c&=\sigma_{\rm LJ}\left[{m(m+1)\zeta(m)\over n(n+1)\zeta(n)}\right]^{1/(m-n)},\\
\lambda_c&=a_c/a_0=[(m+1)/(n+1)]^{1/(m-n)}=1.1077715386\ldots
\end{align}
for $m=12.19,n=6$. With $\lambda=a/a_0$,
\begin{equation}U_\infty=E_0\phi(\lambda)+C,\quad
\phi={\lambda^{-m}\over m(m-n)}-{\lambda^{-n}\over n(m-n)},\quad
\phi'(1)=0,\quad\phi''(1)=1,
\end{equation}
where
$E_0=\varepsilon_{\rm LJ}m(m-n)\zeta(m)(\sigma_{\rm LJ}/a_0)^m
=EA_0a_0$. This proves that $\phi$ is the exactly normalized zeta-lattice
energy, not an empirical deletion of zeta factors.

\section{Statistical ensemble and normalization problem}
$F=\sigma A_0$ has units N and
$\Phi=U-F(a-a_0)$. $A_0$ is a mechanical representative area, neither a
correlation area nor a finite-element area. For tensile $F$, $\Phi\to-\infty$;
for $F=0$, $U\to0$. Thus a one-spacing Gibbs law on $(0,\infty)$ is not
normalizable. We use a declared finite intact basin and conditional Gibbs
initialization. A fixed-total-length canonical chain is an alternative but
couples multiple spacings.

\section{Langevin-to-Smoluchowski derivation}
An isothermal eliminated phonon bath, rapid velocity relaxation, constant
friction and white-noise scale separation give, by fluctuation--dissipation,
\begin{equation}\gamma\dot a=-\Phi_a+\sqrt{2\gamma k_BT}\xi,
\quad\langle\xi(t)\xi(t')\rangle=\delta(t-t').\end{equation}
The forward equation is
\begin{equation}\partial_tP=-\partial_aJ,\qquad
J=-{1\over\gamma}(\Phi_aP+k_BT P_a),\quad
M_a=\gamma^{-1},\quad D_a=k_BT/\gamma.\end{equation}
No empirical diffusion or hysteresis operator is introduced.

\section{Probability current, hysteresis and energy balance}
The maintained observables are
$\int P\dd a=1$, $\bar a=\int aP\dd a$,
$\bar U=\int[U-U(a_0)]P\dd a$, and
$w_h=\int\sigma\dot{\bar\lambda}\dd t$ with
$H_k=\oint_k\sigma\dd\bar\lambda$.
For sinusoidal loading, finite mobility makes $P$ lag the instantaneous
conditional distribution. Loading and unloading distributions differ at the
same stress. Diffusion alone is not the cause; the competition between drive
and relaxation times is. Both frozen very-fast and equilibrated quasistatic
limits have vanishing loops, with a finite-rate maximum possible.

For $\mathcal F=\int P(\Phi+k_BT\ln P)\dd a$,
\begin{equation}\dot{\mathcal F}=\int P\partial_t\Phi\dd a
-\int{J^2\over M_aP}\dd a-[\mu J]_L^R,
\quad \mu=\Phi+k_BT(\ln P+1),\end{equation}
so current dissipation is nonnegative. $H_k$ is work, not stored pair energy.

\section{Irreversible initiation, survival and hazard}
A reflecting process can become periodic without accumulating damage. For
$0<F<F_c$, $U'=F$ also has stable and unstable roots; $a_b(F)>a_c$ is an
alternative energetic barrier and merges with $a_c$ at $F_c$. This initiation
study instead defines crack initiation operationally as first loss of tangent
stability of the local homogeneous environment. The intact domain therefore
has an absorbing boundary exactly at $a_c$:
\begin{equation}S=\int_\Omega\rho\dd a,\quad P_{\rm init}=1-S,
\quad h=J_{\rm out}/S.\end{equation}
The reflecting-model mass above $a_c$ is an instantaneous tail diagnostic;
first-passage flux and cumulative initiation are distinct. No post-initiation
opening or propagation is modeled.

\section{Numerical method and verification}
Cell-centered finite volumes use Chang--Cooper exponential fitting
\cite{changcooper}, backward Euler, reflecting lower flux and selectable
reflecting or absorbing upper boundary. Tests cover pair counting, zeta
convergence, equilibrium and instability derivatives, exact normalized-energy
equivalence, Gibbs stationarity, positivity, reflecting mass, absorbing
outflux balance, phase lag, frequency limits and refinement. Moment balances
retain $-[qJ]_L^R$; conditional moments obey
$\dot{\langle q\rangle}_c=(\dot Q_q-\langle q\rangle_c\dot S)/S$.
Nonlinear LJ drift leaves the hierarchy unclosed; no Gaussian closure is used.

\section{Limitations and unresolved physical parameters}
Unresolved physical inputs include $\varepsilon_{\rm LJ},\sigma_{\rm LJ}$
consistent with the 1D coarse graining, $A_0$, $T$, mobility, escape condition,
and correlation scales $l_c,A_c,V_c$. Physical inputs are separated from
domain, cell count, timestep, tolerance and output cadence. Consequently the
reported sweep is dimensionless and is not a calibrated aluminum lifetime.

\section{FEM/UI extension roadmap}
Only after theory validation, a separate branch may display 1D--3D meshes,
orbit/zoom/pan, loading axis, scalar normal stress, and local density/tail/
initiation maps. Mesh elements are not independent probability samples and no
shear or multiaxial capability is implied.
