$a,a_0$ & local normal spacing and reference spacing & m & $a=a_0\lambda$; $a_0$ is independently calibrated. \\
$\lambda$ & normalized spacing & 1 & $\lambda=a/a_0$. \\
$t,\tau,t_0$ & physical time, reduced time, atomic reference time & s, 1, s & $\tau=t/t_0$, $t_0=\sqrt{m_a a_0/(EA_0)}$. \\
$m_a$ & atomic/reference inertial mass & kg & Enters $t_0$ in the microscopic calibration. \\
$E$ & Young-type normal stiffness bridge & Pa & Used in $q=\sigma_n/E$ and the energy scale $EA_ca_0$. \\
$A_0$ & atomic/reference mechanical area & m$^2$ & Reference area used in the microscopic stiffness calibration; not identified with $A_c$. \\
$\sigma_n,q$ & applied normal stress and reduced traction & Pa, 1 & $q(t)=\sigma_n(t)/E$. \\
$q_{\rm ref}$ & reference reduced traction & 1 & Loading value associated with the structural reference distribution $P_0$. \\
$q_r(\lambda_0)$ & quenched local prestress offset & 1 & $q_r(\lambda_0)=\phi'(\lambda_0)-q_{\rm ref}$. \\
$m,n$ & generalized-LJ exponents & 1 & Active calibration: $m=12.19$, $n=6$, with $m>n$. \\
$\phi,\phi',\phi''$ & normalized interaction, traction and tangent stiffness & 1 & Evaluated directly from the generalized-LJ expressions in the text, without Taylor replacement. \\
$\lambda_c$ & operational normal-instability dividing surface & 1 & $\phi''(\lambda_c)=0$, hence $\lambda_c=[(m+1)/(n+1)]^{1/(m-n)}$. \\
$q_c$ & maximum stable reduced traction & 1 & $q_c=\phi'(\lambda_c)$. \\
$x_j,M$ & normalized node coordinate and number of spacings & 1, 1 & Finite-chain microscopic reference variables. \\
$\lambda_i,c_i$ & spacing and spacing rate of microscopic label $i$ & 1, reduced time$^{-1}$ & $\lambda_i=x_i-x_{i-1}$, $c_i=d\lambda_i/d\tau$. \\
$E_{\rm mech}^*$ & reduced conservative mechanical energy & 1 & $\frac12\sum_j\dot x_j^2+\sum_i\phi(\lambda_i)$. \\
$\Gamma_0,\mu_0,\Phi^q$ & full initial microstate, its measure, and deterministic flow & -- & Used only by the exact microscopic push-forward reference layer. \\
$\Lambda_i$ & microscopic spacing trajectory & 1 & $\Lambda_i(\tau;\Gamma_0,q)$ is generated by the finite chain. \\
$F_M$ & empirical spacing--rate phase-space measure & -- & $M^{-1}\sum_i\delta(\lambda-\lambda_i)\delta(c-c_i)$. \\
$P$ & normalized spacing density / measure & 1 per $\lambda$ & $P=\int F\,dc$; its form is mechanically generated. \\
$u$ & conditional mean spacing rate & reduced time$^{-1}$ & $u=\mathrm E[c\mid\lambda,\tau]$. \\
$\Theta$ & conditional spacing-rate variance & reduced time$^{-2}$ & $\Theta=\mathrm{Var}(c\mid\lambda,\tau)$. \\
$C_3$ & third conditional central spacing-rate moment & reduced time$^{-3}$ & $C_3=\mathrm E[(c-u)^3\mid\lambda,\tau]$. \\
$\Psi$ & conditional rate--acceleration covariance & reduced time$^{-3}$ & $\Psi=\mathrm{Cov}(c,\ddot\lambda\mid\lambda,\tau)$. \\
$\mathcal A$ & conditional mean spacing acceleration & reduced time$^{-2}$ & $\mathcal A=\mathrm E[\ddot\lambda_i\mid\lambda_i=\lambda,\tau]$. \\
$D_\tau$ & material derivative in spacing space & reduced time$^{-1}$ & $D_\tau=\partial_\tau+u\partial_\lambda$. \\
$J,Q_*$ & probability flux and instantaneous upper-tail mass & -- & $J=Pu$, $Q_*=\int_{\lambda_*}^{\infty}P\,d\lambda$. \\
$J_+,J_-$ & outward and return threshold fluxes & time$^{-1}$ & Positive/negative velocity parts of the phase-space flux at a threshold. \\
$P_0(\lambda)$ & structural/prestress initial spacing density & 1 per $\lambda$ & Required reduced-model initial condition; not an assumed named PDF family. \\
$\Lambda(\lambda_0,t)$ & reduced quasistatic characteristic & 1 & Stable root of $\phi'(\Lambda)=\phi'(\lambda_0)+q(t)-q_{\rm ref}$. \\
$\Delta\psi_c$ & reduced effective-potential climb to $\lambda_c$ & 1 & $[\phi(\lambda_c)-\phi'(\lambda)\lambda_c]-[\phi(\lambda)-\phi'(\lambda)\lambda]$. \\
$A_c$ & characteristic cohesive area of one local rare event & m$^2$ & Kept explicit; must be independently calibrated or computed. \\
$\Delta G_c$ & local event energy climb & J & $\Delta G_c=EA_ca_0\Delta\psi_c$. \\
$T,k_B$ & temperature and Boltzmann constant & K, J K$^{-1}$ & Enter the finite-temperature transition-state factor. \\
$k_c$ & positive-flux transition-state escape rate & s$^{-1}$ & $\sqrt{\phi''}/(2\pi t_0)\exp[-\Delta G_c/(k_BT)]$ within the declared rare-event/harmonic-well approximation. \\
$P_b$ & intact survivor subdensity & 1 per $\lambda$ & Evolves by quasistatic transport plus the sink $-k_cP_b$. \\
$S,F_{\rm ci}$ & local survival and cumulative initiation probability & 1 & $S=\int P_b\,d\lambda$, $F_{\rm ci}=1-S$. \\
$T_f$ & fatigue-loading period & s & $T_f=1/f$ for periodic loading. \\
$\mathcal H_c$ & one-cycle integrated local hazard & 1 & $\int_0^{T_f}k_c[\Lambda(\lambda_0,t),T;A_c]dt$. \\
$N,S_N$ & cycle count and local survival after $N$ cycles & 1 & $S_N=\int P_0(\lambda_0)e^{-N\mathcal H_c(\lambda_0)}d\lambda_0$. \\
$R$ & characteristic-area ratio & 1 & $R=A_c/A_0$; used only for sensitivity sweeps, not adopted as a calibrated value. \\
$\bar a,\bar U,U_{\rm ref}$ & mean spacing, mean intrinsic energy, energy reference scale & m, J, J & Direct moments of the current spacing density. \\
$s,b,U_0(a,s)$ & registry extension coordinate, registry period, and multilayer energy & m, m, J & Non-mainline plasticity/defect extension only. \\
$\dot D_{\rm irr},E_{\rm hyst}$ & irreversible dissipation rate and cumulative hysteresis energy & J s$^{-1}$, J & Set to zero in the conservative microscopic baseline; not inferred from first passage. \\
