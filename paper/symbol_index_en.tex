\begin{longtable}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.13\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.23\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.13\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.32\textwidth}}

\endfirsthead\toprule Symbol & Physical meaning & Unit & Definition, evaluation, and location\\ \midrule\endhead\bottomrule\endfoot
$a,a_0$ & spacing and reference spacing & m & State coordinate and reference in Sec.~\ref{sec:decomp}.\\
$a^\ddagger$ & outer normal saddle & m & Negative-curvature root of Eq.~\eqref{eq:normal-barrier}; bracket the full derivative.\\
$b$ & row repeat/slip period & m & Declared crystallographic repeat in Sec.~\ref{sec:row}.\\
$s,s_0,\widetilde s$ & unwrapped, reference, and intrawell registry & m & $s=s_0+zb+\widetilde s$, Sec.~\ref{sec:plastic}.\\
$z$ & unwrapped well index & 1 & Integer basin label; compute before folding $s$.\\
$m,n,q$ & LJ and generic exponents & 1 & $m>n>2$ for $U_0$; $q>1$ suffices for one row.\\
$\epsLJ,\sigLJ$ & pair well depth and zero crossing & J, m & Independent pair parameters in Eq.~\eqref{eq:pair}.\\
$C_{m,n}$ & well-depth normalization & 1 & Closed expression in Eq.~\eqref{eq:pair}.\\
$p,k,\ell$ & row-site, normal-layer, reciprocal indices & 1 & $p\in\mathbb Z$, $k,\ell\ge1$; Secs.~\ref{sec:row}--\ref{sec:H}.\\
$\delta,\delta_0,\eta$ & normalized registries and spacing & 1 & $s/b,s_0/b,a/b$.\\
$B_q$ & one-row inverse-power kernel & 1 & Direct Eq.~\eqref{eq:Bdirect}; Bessel Eq.~\eqref{eq:Bbessel}.\\
$H_q$ & unweighted multilayer sum & 1 & Direct Eq.~\eqref{eq:Hdirect}; exact series Eq.~\eqref{eq:Hexact}.\\
$\Delta H_q$ & registry excess of $H_q$ & 1 & Cosine-difference series Eq.~\eqref{eq:DeltaH}.\\
$K_\nu$ & modified Bessel function, second kind & 1 & Integral definition Eq.~\eqref{eq:Kdef}; standard special-function evaluation.\\
$\mathcal K_\nu$ & Bessel--Lambert series & 1 & $\sum k^{-\nu}K_\nu(kx)$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:Klambert}.\\
$\operatorname{Li}_r$ & polylogarithm & 1 & $\sum e^{-kx}/k^r$; 12--6 closure in Sec.~\ref{sec:polylog}.\\
$v_{m,n}$ & pair potential & J & Eq.~\eqref{eq:pair}; pair counted once.\\
$W(d,s)$ & one row--row kernel & J & Direct site sum Eq.~\eqref{eq:W}; not the final lattice energy.\\
$U_0$ & intrinsic multilayer potential & J & Unweighted $\sum_kW(ka,s)$, Eqs.~\eqref{eq:U0count} and \eqref{eq:U0H}; excludes external work.\\
$\begin{gathered}\Delta U_0,\Delta U_n\\V_{\rm slip}\end{gathered}$ & total excess, normal part, slip part & J & Exact common-energy identity Eq.~\eqref{eq:decomp}.\\
$P$ & spacing--registry density & m$^{-2}$ & Underlying continuity law Eq.~\eqref{eq:smol}.\\
$J_a,J_s$ & probability currents & m$^{-1}$s$^{-1}$ & Force/diffusion currents in Eq.~\eqref{eq:currents}.\\
$M_a,M_s$ & constant mobilities & m$^2$J$^{-1}$s$^{-1}$ & Long-time overdamped coefficients; Einstein diffusion is $k_BT M_i$.\\
$Q_a,Q_s$ & tensile-derived generalized forces & N & $A_0\sigma$ and $A_0M\sigma$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:driving}.\\
$M$ & signed Schmid factor & 1 & $(\bm e\cdot\bm n)(\bm e\cdot\bm d)$ for one declared slip system.\\
$A_0$ & representative mechanical area & m$^2$ & Converts stress to generalized force; not a FEM or correlation area.\\
$T,k_B$ & bath temperature and Boltzmann constant & K, J K$^{-1}$ & Isothermal fluctuation--dissipation assumption.\\
$\bar a,\bar U$ & G1 mean spacing and G2 mean intrinsic excess & m, J & Eqs.~\eqref{eq:G1}--\eqref{eq:G2}.\\
$\dot D_{\rm irr},E_{\rm hyst}$ & dissipation rate and cumulative hysteresis & J s$^{-1}$, J & G3, Eq.~\eqref{eq:G3}; not automatically stored damage.\\
$S,P_{\rm crack}$ & survival and initiation probability & 1 & G4 extension, Eq.~\eqref{eq:G4escape}.\\
$h_{\rm slip}$ & slip homogenization thickness & m & Independently specified mapping scale, Eq.~\eqref{eq:plastic-strain}.\\
$\gamma_p,\varepsilon_p$ & plastic shear and tensile strain & 1 & Derived from residual $\langle z\rangle$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:plastic-strain}.\\
$\Omega_b$ & bonded state domain & m$^2$ & Reflecting or absorbing domain in Sec.~\ref{sec:crack}.\\
$\omega,\sigma_m,\sigma_a$ & angular frequency, mean and amplitude & s$^{-1}$, Pa, Pa & Uniaxial waveform Eq.~\eqref{eq:driving}.\\
