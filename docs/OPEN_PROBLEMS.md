## September 2026 periodic-first-passage update

The mathematical connection from the active absorbing Smoluchowski PDE to
long-cycle survival is now closed at the model level. The one-cycle positive
sub-Markov operator has principal multiplier $r$, giving integrated cycle
hazard $-\log r$, principal-mode survival $S_N=r^N$, an adjoint-derived
initial-state prefactor, and controlled fast/slow frequency limits. See
`SMOLUCHOWSKI_FLOQUET_SURVIVAL.md`.

What remains open is physical identification, not another empirical life law:
$A_0$, spacing mobility/friction, bath reduction, single-crystal directional

$\lambda_c$ as an initiation event.

## Scope

The active theory is strictly one-dimensional, normal-only, and layer based. The effective normal interaction is the calibrated generalized Lennard-Jones model. Archived 3D FCC and shear work are not part of the active derivation.

## 1. Derive the mechanically generated spatial state before choosing another probability family

The one-point density is exactly the spatial push-forward

$$
p_\lambda(\lambda,t)
=
\int_0^1\delta[\lambda-\Lambda(\xi,t)]\,d\xi.
$$

Therefore the highest-priority problem is no longer to select another named density family. It is to determine the mechanically excited spatial waveform or equivalent pair state from the 1D governing equation

$$
\ddot\lambda_i
=
\phi'(\lambda_{i+1})
-2\phi'(\lambda_i)
+\phi'(\lambda_{i-1}).
$$

The target is a minimum representation that reproduces both $P_1(\lambda,t)$ and the observed spatial correlation $C_k(t)$.

## 2. Derive the exact neighboring-spacing hierarchy

Define an adjacent-spacing joint density

$$
P_2(\lambda,\lambda',t).
$$

The next derivation must show exactly how its evolution depends on higher-order information and identify where a $P_3$-type object first enters. No empirical correlation length or fitted relaxation law may be inserted before this hierarchy is explicit.

## 3. Determine whether a low-dimensional mode representation is sufficient

Linearized mechanics gives

$$
\omega_q^2=4\sin^2(q/2),
$$

and a single mode produces an arcsine push-forward density. The existing driven snapshots falsify single-mode sufficiency, and the slower snapshot has skewness larger than the maximum possible for a first-plus-second-harmonic waveform.

The next test is whether a small mechanically selected set of modes can reproduce simultaneously:

1. the empirical one-point distribution;
2. the observed skewness and higher moments;
3. the scaled correlation profile $\rho_k$ versus $k/M$.

## 4. Derive the time law for stored configurational energy

The theory still needs

$$
\boxed{
\sigma_n(t)
\rightarrow
W(t)
\rightarrow
\mathcal E(t)
}
$$

from an explicit energy balance without a fitted retained-energy fraction. Kinetic energy, reversible mean deformation energy, and redistribution energy must remain separated.

## 5. Determine the exact role of external stress in the probability hierarchy

A candidate one-point force moment is

$$
\frac{1}{A_0}\int U'(a)P_a(a,t)\,da.
$$

Its exact relation to the externally applied stress depends on the boundary-value problem and must be derived from the reduced mechanics rather than imposed as an independent fit constraint.

## 6. Compression-side closure for the exact energy-feasibility theorem

Normalization, mean, and energy alone cannot force a tensile tail because LJ compression can carry unbounded energy as $\lambda\to0^+$.

An exact crack-free energy ceiling therefore still requires an independently justified compression-side constraint, such as a mechanically derived lower support bound or one-sided compression-energy budget.

## 7. Tail versus first passage — mathematical link completed, physics open

The instantaneous tail

$$
Q_c(t)=\int_{\lambda_c}^{\infty}p_\lambda(\lambda,t)\,d\lambda
$$

is not a cumulative crack-initiation probability. The active kinetic model now
keeps it as a reflecting precursor diagnostic and separately defines
irreversible initiation as absorbing first passage through $\lambda_c$. Its
cycle operator supplies survival, flux and hazard without fitting. The open
question is whether this operational tangent-instability crossing agrees with
atomistic or experimental crack-initiation observations for the chosen
single-crystal direction and representative patch.

## 8. Required falsification rules

Any next model must preserve:

1. normalization and positivity of probability states;
2. fixed calibrated LJ parameters through time;
3. explicit separation of exact identities, assumptions, controlled approximations, and numerical diagnostics;
4. direct comparison with deterministic 1D mechanics whenever a probability form is proposed;
5. convergence/conservation checks where applicable;
6. no fitted Gaussian, Weibull, empirical damage law, relaxation time, or correlation length used as a substitute for derivation;
7. failed candidate forms retained as results rather than tuned away.

## 범위

활성 이론은 엄격하게 1차원, normal-only, layer 기반이다. 유효 normal interaction은 calibration된 generalized Lennard-Jones model이다. archive된 3D FCC와 shear 연구는 active derivation에 포함하지 않는다.

## 1. 다른 probability family를 고르기 전에 mechanically generated spatial state를 유도한다

one-point density는 정확히

$$
p_\lambda(\lambda,t)
=
\int_0^1\delta[\lambda-\Lambda(\xi,t)]\,d\xi
$$

라는 spatial push-forward다.

따라서 현재 최우선 문제는 새로운 named density family를 고르는 것이 아니다. 1D 지배방정식

$$
\ddot\lambda_i
=
\phi'(\lambda_{i+1})
-2\phi'(\lambda_i)
+\phi'(\lambda_{i-1})
$$

에서 mechanically excited spatial waveform 또는 동등한 pair state를 결정하는 것이다.

목표는 $P_1(\lambda,t)$과 관찰된 spatial correlation $C_k(t)$를 동시에 재현하는 최소 representation을 찾는 것이다.

## 2. 정확한 neighboring-spacing hierarchy를 유도한다

adjacent-spacing joint density

$$
P_2(\lambda,\lambda',t)
$$

를 정의한다.

다음 derivation에서는 그 evolution이 higher-order information에 정확히 어떻게 의존하는지 보이고, $P_3$형 object가 처음 어디서 등장하는지 밝혀야 한다. 이 hierarchy가 명확해지기 전에 empirical correlation length나 fitted relaxation law를 넣지 않는다.

## 3. Low-dimensional mode representation이 충분한지 확인한다

선형화된 mechanics는

$$
\omega_q^2=4\sin^2(q/2)
$$

를 주고 single mode는 arcsine push-forward density를 만든다. 기존 driven snapshot은 single-mode sufficiency를 반증했고, 느린 snapshot의 skewness는 first+second harmonic waveform이 낼 수 있는 최대값보다도 크다.

다음 시험에서는 작은 수의 mechanically selected mode가 동시에 다음을 재현할 수 있는지 본다.

1. empirical one-point distribution;
2. 관찰된 skewness와 higher moment;
3. $k/M$에 대한 scaled correlation profile $\rho_k$.

## 4. Stored configurational energy의 시간법칙을 유도한다

여전히 fitted retained-energy fraction 없이 explicit energy balance로

$$
\boxed{
\sigma_n(t)
\rightarrow
W(t)
\rightarrow
\mathcal E(t)
}
$$

를 유도해야 한다. kinetic energy, reversible mean deformation energy, redistribution energy를 분리해야 한다.

## 5. Probability hierarchy에서 external stress의 정확한 역할을 유도한다

candidate one-point force moment는

$$
\frac{1}{A_0}\int U'(a)P_a(a,t)\,da
$$

이다.

이 식과 externally applied stress 사이의 정확한 관계는 boundary-value problem에 의존하므로 independent fit constraint로 부과하지 말고 reduced mechanics에서 유도해야 한다.

## 6. Exact energy-feasibility theorem의 compression-side closure

normalization, mean, energy만으로는 tensile tail을 강제할 수 없다. LJ compression은 $\lambda\to0^+$에서 무한한 energy를 담을 수 있기 때문이다.

따라서 exact crack-free energy ceiling에는 mechanically derived lower support bound 또는 one-sided compression-energy budget 같은 독립적으로 정당화된 compression-side constraint가 여전히 필요하다.

## 7. Tail과 first passage 구분 — 수학적 연결 완료, 물리 검증 미완료

instantaneous tail

$$
Q_c(t)=\int_{\lambda_c}^{\infty}p_\lambda(\lambda,t)\,d\lambda
$$

은 cumulative crack-initiation probability가 아니다. 현재 kinetic model은
이를 reflecting precursor 진단량으로만 유지하고, 비가역 개시는
$\lambda_c$ 흡수경계의 first passage로 별도 정의했다. 한 주기
연산자에서 생존, 유출, hazard가 fitting 없이 유도된다. 남은 문제는 이
변곡점 통과 정의가 선택한 단결정 방향과 대표 patch에서 atomistic/실험
균열개시와 일치하는지 검증하는 것이다.
