# Fatigue mechanism assessment: bounded PART I–III conclusion

20 October 2026. A full mechanism reconstruction (directive PART IV onward)
is **deferred**, as requested for the first delivery. This note records what
the completed foundations do and do not imply; it supplies no new fitted
damage law or fatigue-life prediction.

The counted Bessel energy can describe a reversible rigid cut path under
declared geometry. The PMF can add relaxation and equilibrium hidden-state
entropy. An exact reduced equation also contains preparation-dependent
fluctuating force and memory. None of these statements identifies an
irreversible crack-initiation mechanism in a real specimen by itself.

An absorbing first-opening event is a mathematically useful stopping rule.
Its cumulative absorbed mass can increase even for a time-independent
potential with stationary dynamics. That increase proves the event
definition and transport bookkeeping, not accumulation of microstructural
fatigue damage before the event. Mapping it to an observed crack needs an
independent detection/size/coalescence criterion and synchronized load data.

Periodic tangential registry allows repeated slip paths. It does not by
itself prove dislocation nucleation, persistent slip bands, residual stress,
plastic hysteresis or irreversible damage. A position-only Markov state
((a,s)) can be adequate only if the hidden variables needed for its future
conditional law have relaxed or are encoded by that state. If, for example,
two atomistic preparations at the same ((a,s)) but different defect
structures have different future opening statistics, that closure fails.
The remedy would be tested extra variables or memory, not an arbitrary
cycle-dependent barrier chosen to produce a desired curve.

The first experiments should therefore distinguish:

| Question | Required comparison | Present status |
|---|---|---|
| Is the rigid cut a good thermodynamic coordinate? | Same-coordinate constrained ensemble and PMF versus $W_\Gamma$, at multiple $T$ and states | Not established for the adopted cut |
| Is its reduced law Markov and overdamped? | Conditional forces/velocities, memory spectrum, preparation and load-frequency tests | Mathematical counterexamples and limit controls completed; material gate open |
| Is first opening an irreversible material event? | Atomistic recrossing, relaxation, defect evolution and event-size records | Not supplied by a sink convention |
| Does local event mass predict detected initiation? | Spatial/coalescence model and specimen observation calibration | Outside this delivery |

The full assessment of slip/defects, irreversible evolution, spatial coupling
and observed fatigue will require evidence beyond PART I–III. The present
decision is to keep Rev.11 as a clearly conditional framework and use the
derived PMF/memory route to decide which closure the material supports.
