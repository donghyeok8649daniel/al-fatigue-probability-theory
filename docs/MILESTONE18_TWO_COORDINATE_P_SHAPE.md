# Milestone 18 — Exact two-coordinate shape constraint for $P(a,s,t)$

## Scope

This milestone extends the previously derived one-spacing $\Theta$-based density identity to the active reduced state

$$
\mathbf x=(a,s),
$$

where $a$ is the collective normal layer spacing and $s$ is the unwrapped collective registry coordinate.

No Boltzmann distribution, Gaussian/Weibull family, Fokker--Planck equation, Markov bath, white-noise forcing, spacing independence, or registry independence is assumed in the derivation below.

The result is an **EXACT SMOOTH-MOMENT IDENTITY** under the declared regularity conditions. It is not yet a closed predictive kinetic law because the conditional acceleration, conditional velocity covariance, and material acceleration must be generated by the mechanics.

---

## 1. Empirical state density

For represented states $\alpha=1,\dots,M$,

$$
\mathbf x_\alpha(t)=
\begin{bmatrix}
a_\alpha(t)\\
s_\alpha(t)
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\mathbf v_\alpha(t)=\dot{\mathbf x}_\alpha(t),
$$

define

$$
P_M(a,s,t)
=
\frac1M\sum_{\alpha=1}^{M}
\delta[a-a_\alpha(t)]\delta[s-s_\alpha(t)].
$$

This is an exact empirical measure. A smooth $P(a,s,t)$ is its continuum representation when such a representation is meaningful.

No statistical independence between represented states is required by this definition.

---

## 2. Probability current and continuity

Define

$$
J_i(\mathbf x,t)=P(\mathbf x,t)u_i(\mathbf x,t),
$$

with

$$
u_i(\mathbf x,t)
=
\mathbb E[v_i\mid\mathbf x].
$$

Then

$$
\boxed{
\partial_tP+\nabla\cdot(P\mathbf u)=0
}
$$

is the exact one-point continuity identity.

This is the transport basis of G4. For no-flux boundaries it preserves

$$
\iint P\,da\,ds=1.
$$

For an absorbing crack boundary $\Gamma_{\rm crack}$,

$$
-\dot S
=
\int_{\Gamma_{\rm crack}}\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf n\,d\Gamma,
\qquad
P_{\rm crack}=1-S.
$$

---

## 3. Conditional velocity covariance

The scalar one-dimensional $\Theta$ becomes the tensor

$$
\boxed{
\boldsymbol\Theta(a,s,t)
=
\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf v\mid a,s)
=
\begin{bmatrix}
\Theta_{aa} & \Theta_{as}\\
\Theta_{as} & \Theta_{ss}
\end{bmatrix}
}
$$

with

$$
\Theta_{aa}=\operatorname{Var}(\dot a\mid a,s),
$$

$$
\Theta_{ss}=\operatorname{Var}(\dot s\mid a,s),
$$

and

$$
\boxed{
\Theta_{as}=\operatorname{Cov}(\dot a,\dot s\mid a,s).
}
$$

$\Theta_{as}$ must not be set to zero without a physical or numerical demonstration. It is the direct second-moment measure of opening--registry dynamical correlation in the reduced state.

---

## 4. Conditional acceleration

To avoid conflict with the governing observable $\bar a(t)$, define

$$
\boxed{
\boldsymbol{\mathcal A}(a,s,t)
=
\mathbb E[\ddot{\mathbf x}\mid a,s]
}
$$

rather than using $\bar a$ for conditional acceleration.

The first velocity-moment balance is

$$
\boxed{
\partial_t(Pu_i)
+
\partial_j\left[P(u_i u_j+\Theta_{ij})\right]
=
P\mathcal A_i.
}
$$

This is the two-coordinate counterpart of the one-spacing moment equation.

---

## 5. Exact density log-gradient

Using continuity to remove the mean-flow transport terms gives

$$
D_tu_i
=
\mathcal A_i
-
\frac1P\partial_j(P\Theta_{ij}),
$$

where

$$
D_t=\partial_t+u_a\partial_a+u_s\partial_s.
$$

Expanding the covariance-flux term,

$$
\mathcal A_i-D_tu_i
=
\partial_j\Theta_{ij}
+
\Theta_{ij}\partial_j\ln P.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{
\boldsymbol\Theta\nabla\ln P
=
\boldsymbol{\mathcal A}
-D_t\mathbf u
-\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\Theta.
}
$$

Where $P>0$ and $\boldsymbol\Theta$ is positive definite,

$$
\boxed{
\nabla\ln P
=
\boldsymbol\Theta^{-1}
\left[
\boldsymbol{\mathcal A}
-D_t\mathbf u
-\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\Theta
\right].
}
$$

Classification: **EXACT SMOOTH-MOMENT IDENTITY under the stated regularity and invertibility conditions.**

It is not a Fokker--Planck closure and it does not prescribe a named PDF family.

---

## 6. Compatibility condition unique to the two-coordinate form

Define

$$
\mathbf g
=
\boldsymbol\Theta^{-1}
\left[
\boldsymbol{\mathcal A}
-D_t\mathbf u
-\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\Theta
\right].
$$

For a scalar density to exist with

$$
\mathbf g=\nabla\ln P,
$$

the field must be locally integrable. On a simply connected $(a,s)$ domain,

$$
\boxed{
\partial_a g_s-\partial_s g_a=0.
}
$$

This gives a new numerical/closure diagnostic:

- exact smooth fields must satisfy the condition;
- a nonzero residual can identify finite-sample noise, numerical differentiation error, or a mutually inconsistent closure for $\Theta$, $\mathcal A$, and $D_t\mathbf u$.

This compatibility test should be evaluated before interpreting a reconstructed two-dimensional density physically.

---

## 7. Relation to the intrinsic lattice energy

The active intrinsic energy remains

$$
U_0(a,s)
=
\sum_{k\ge1}\sum_{p\in\mathbb Z}
v_{m,n}\!\left(\sqrt{k^2a^2+(pb+s)^2}\right).
$$

Its generalized intrinsic forces are

$$
-\partial_aU_0,
\qquad
-\partial_sU_0.
$$

The present density identity does **not** assume

$$
P\propto e^{-U_0/k_BT}.
$$

Instead $U_0$ enters through the actual mechanics that generate $\boldsymbol{\mathcal A}$, $\mathbf u$, and $\boldsymbol\Theta$. If the reduced equation of motion is later written schematically as

$$
\mathbf M\ddot{\mathbf x}
=
\mathbf Q(t)-\nabla U_0+\mathbf R,
$$

then

$$
\boldsymbol{\mathcal A}
=
\mathbf M^{-1}
\left[
\mathbf Q-\nabla U_0+\mathbb E(\mathbf R\mid a,s)
\right].
$$

The unresolved force $\mathbf R$ must not be silently set to zero unless the selected mechanics actually justify it.

## 8. Connection to the four governing relations

Once $P(a,s,t)$ is available, the fixed four governing relations remain unchanged.

### G1 — mean spacing

$$
\boxed{
\bar a(t)=\iint aP(a,s,t)\,da\,ds.
}
$$

### G2 — mean intrinsic lattice energy

$$
\boxed{
\bar U(t)
=
\iint \Delta U_0(a,s)P(a,s,t)\,da\,ds.
}
$$

This is the central ensemble energy observable. $U_0(a,s)$ is the microscopic landscape; $\bar U(t)$ is the macroscopic ensemble-average intrinsic energy.

### G3 — cumulative irreversible/hysteretic energy

$$
\boxed{
E_{\rm hyst}(t)=\int_0^t\dot D_{\rm irr}(t')dt'.
}
$$

The exact density shape identity alone does **not** determine $\dot D_{\rm irr}$. A concrete irreversible mechanism must be declared before a specific dissipation formula is used.

### G4 — normalization/survival

$$
\boxed{
\iint P\,da\,ds=1
}
$$

for a closed intact domain, or

$$
\boxed{
S(t)=\iint_{\Omega_b}P\,da\,ds,\qquad
P_{\rm crack}=1-S
}
$$

with an absorbing crack boundary.

## 9. Plasticity from the same density

Keep $s$ unwrapped:

$$
s=s_0+zb+\tilde s,
\qquad z\in\mathbb Z.
$$

For registry well $\mathcal W_z$,

$$
p_z(t)=\iint_{\mathcal W_z}P(a,s,t)\,da\,ds,
$$

and

$$
\boxed{
\langle z\rangle=\sum_z z p_z.
}
$$

Therefore

$$
\gamma_p=\frac{b}{h_{\rm slip}}\langle z\rangle,
\qquad
\varepsilon_p=M\gamma_p.
$$

Plasticity is thus read from inter-well probability transfer in the same $P(a,s,t)$ rather than from an independently fitted plastic-strain law.

---

## 10. Validity ledger

The following assumptions/conditions must be checked whenever this identity is used:

1. a smooth one-point phase-space representation exists over the evaluated region;
2. the required first and second conditional velocity moments are finite;
3. velocity-space boundary terms vanish when deriving the moment equations;
4. $P>0$ for the divided log-density form;
5. $\boldsymbol\Theta$ is positive definite for direct inversion;
6. if $\boldsymbol\Theta$ becomes singular, the undivided matrix relation must be used instead;
7. a physical prediction still requires a mechanics-based determination of $\boldsymbol{\mathcal A}$, $\mathbf u$, and $\boldsymbol\Theta$;
8. G3 additionally requires a declared irreversible mechanism.

Not assumed: Boltzmann equilibrium, Gaussian/Weibull density, Fokker--Planck dynamics, Markovianity, white noise, or independent layer states.

# 한국어 요약

현재 활성 상태 $\mathbf x=(a,s)$에서는 기존 1D $\Theta$가

$$
\boldsymbol\Theta=
\begin{bmatrix}
\Theta_{aa}&\Theta_{as}\\
\Theta_{as}&\Theta_{ss}
\end{bmatrix}
$$

라는 조건부 속도 공분산 텐서로 확장된다. exact smooth-moment balance에서

$$
\boxed{
\nabla\ln P
=
\boldsymbol\Theta^{-1}
[\boldsymbol{\mathcal A}-D_t\mathbf u-\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\Theta]
}
$$

를 얻는다. 이 식은 Boltzmann이나 Fokker--Planck를 가정해서 얻은 식이 아니며, 실제 mechanics가 만들어내는 conditional acceleration과 velocity statistics가 PDF의 형태를 결정한다.

2D에서는 추가로

$$
\boxed{
\partial_a g_s-\partial_s g_a=0
}
$$

이라는 compatibility condition이 생긴다. 이는 향후 $(a,s)$ numerical model에서 매우 중요한 self-consistency test로 사용한다.

동일한 $P(a,s,t)$에서 G1의 평균 spacing, G2의 평균 intrinsic energy, registry-well population에 의한 plasticity, G4의 survival을 계산한다. G3의 irreversible dissipation은 이 exact kinematic identity만으로 정해지지 않으므로 별도의 물리적 비가역 mechanism을 먼저 확정해야 한다.
