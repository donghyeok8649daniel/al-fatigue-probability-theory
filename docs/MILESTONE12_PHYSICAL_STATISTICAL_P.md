# Milestone 12 — Physical Statistical-Mechanical Form of the 1D Layer-Spacing Distribution

## Scope

This milestone asks a narrower question than the previous transport work:

> **What functional form for $P(\lambda,t)$ is implied by established physical theory when the full nonlinear layer-LJ Hamiltonian is placed in a clearly specified mechanical/statistical ensemble?**

No Taylor expansion of the LJ potential, finite-harmonic ansatz, Gaussian/Weibull fit, empirical fatigue variable, fitted relaxation time, or arbitrary escape prefactor is introduced.

The answer is not one universal distribution. The governing physics distinguishes at least three regimes:

1. zero-temperature quasistatic homogeneous mechanics;
2. isolated conservative statistical mechanics at fixed total energy and length;
3. thermal/local-equilibrium mechanics at fixed length or inside a metastable tensile basin.

The ensemble and time-scale assumptions are therefore part of the physical statement and may not be hidden.

## 1. Full nonlinear reduced layer potential

The normalized effective layer-spacing coordinate is

$$
\lambda=\frac{a}{a_0}>0,
$$

and the active reduced potential is

$$
\phi(\lambda)
=
\frac{\lambda^{-m}}{m(m-n)}
-
\frac{\lambda^{-n}}{n(m-n)},
$$

with $m=12.19$ and $n=6$ in the current calibration.

It satisfies

$$
\phi'(1)=0,
\qquad
\phi''(1)=1.
$$

No expansion about $\lambda=1$ is used below.

## 2. Physical energy scale implied by the elastic calibration

Write the physical effective energy of one represented layer patch as

$$
U(a)=E_0\phi(a/a_0)+C.
$$

Because

$$
\frac{d^2U}{da^2}
=
\frac{E_0}{a_0^2}\phi''(\lambda),
$$

and the calibration uses

$$
E=\frac{a_0}{A_0}U''(a_0),
$$

with $\phi''(1)=1$, one obtains the **EXACT calibration identity**

$$
\boxed{E_0=EA_0a_0.}
$$

Therefore the physical inverse reduced temperature is

$$
\boxed{
\chi
=\frac{E_0}{k_BT}
=\frac{EA_0a_0}{k_BT}.
}
$$

This exposes a critical physical input that had previously been hidden in purely dimensionless calculations: the representative layer-patch area $A_0$. Until the coarse-graining meaning and crystallographic orientation of $A_0$ are fixed, a numerical value of $\chi$ must **not** be advertised as an aluminum thermal prediction.

Classification: **EXACT / IDENTITY** for the stated coarse-grained energy definition and elastic calibration; $A_0$ itself remains a required **PHYSICAL INPUT**.

## 3. Mechanical force bias

Let the physical tensile force carried by the represented patch be

$$
F=A_0\sigma.
$$

The external work contribution is $-Fa=-Fa_0\lambda$. Since $E_0=EA_0a_0$,

$$
\frac{Fa_0}{E_0}
=\frac{\sigma}{E}
\equiv f.
$$

Thus the exact reduced force-biased potential is

$$
\boxed{
w_f(\lambda)=\phi(\lambda)-f\lambda,
\qquad
f=\frac{\sigma}{E}.
}
$$

Stationary homogeneous spacings satisfy

$$
\boxed{
\phi'(\lambda)=f.
}
$$

For $0<f<f_c$, there are two tensile stationary points:

$$
1<\lambda_s(f)<\lambda_c<\lambda_b(f),
$$

with

$$
\phi''(\lambda_s)>0,
\qquad
\phi''(\lambda_b)<0.
$$

At $f=f_c$ the two stationary points coalesce at $\lambda_c$.

## 4. Zero-temperature quasistatic mechanics forces a delta distribution

This is the first strong physical result for the form of $P$.

### 4.1 Force-controlled statement

At zero temperature, for a spatially homogeneous quasistatic intact state under $0\le f<f_c$, every equivalent layer spacing occupies the stable minimum of $w_f$:

$$
\boxed{
P_{T=0,\mathrm{qs}}(\lambda\mid f)
=
\delta[\lambda-\lambda_s(f)].
}
$$

This is an **EXACT equilibrium statement** for the homogeneous reduced model. Applying it instantaneously to a finite-rate driven chain is a **QUASISTATIC APPROXIMATION**.

### 4.2 Fixed-length convex-branch statement

Suppose the total normalized length fixes the mean spacing $\mu$ and all intact spacings remain inside a strictly convex branch of $\phi$. Then Jensen's inequality gives

$$
\frac1M\sum_{i=1}^M\phi(\lambda_i)
\ge
\phi\!\left(\frac1M\sum_i\lambda_i\right)
=\phi(\mu),
$$

with equality only for

$$
\lambda_1=\cdots=\lambda_M=\mu
$$

when the convexity is strict. Hence the zero-temperature energy minimizer again has

$$
\boxed{P(\lambda)=\delta(\lambda-\mu).}
$$

This proves that a broad smooth $P$ is **not** generated by the homogeneous zero-temperature equilibrium mechanics itself. Broadening requires kinetic excitation, thermal/internal-energy fluctuations, spatially nonuniform dynamics, heterogeneity, or another explicitly represented physical mechanism.

## 5. Isolated conservative chain: physical microcanonical measure

For an isolated conservative chain with total physical energy $E_{\rm tot}$, fixed total normalized length $L$, and $d$ independent quadratic momentum degrees of freedom, the microcanonical phase-space density is

$$
\rho_{\rm mc}(\boldsymbol\lambda,\mathbf p)
\propto
\delta\!\left(
E_{\rm tot}
-K(\mathbf p)
-E_0\sum_{i=1}^M\phi(\lambda_i)
\right)
\delta\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^M\lambda_i-L\right).
$$

Integrating the quadratic momenta produces the configurational weight

$$
\boxed{
\Pi_{\rm mc}(\boldsymbol\lambda\mid E_{\rm tot},L)
\propto
\delta\!\left(\sum_i\lambda_i-L\right)
\left[
E_{\rm tot}-E_0\sum_i\phi(\lambda_i)
\right]_+^{d/2-1}.
}
$$

This is the physically standard microcanonical density of states for the stated Hamiltonian. It corrects an important weakness of the earlier equal-configurational-weight construction: after momenta are integrated out, configurations with different remaining kinetic-energy volume do **not** in general receive equal weight.

The exact one-spacing marginal is therefore

$$
\boxed{
P_M^{\rm mc}(\lambda\mid E_{\rm tot},L)
\propto
\int d\lambda_{2:M}\,
\delta\!\left(
\sum_{j=2}^M\lambda_j-(L-\lambda)
\right)
\left[
E_{\rm tot}
-E_0\phi(\lambda)
-E_0\sum_{j=2}^M\phi(\lambda_j)
\right]_+^{d/2-1}.
}
$$

Classification: **EXACT EQUILIBRIUM STATISTICAL-MECHANICS FORM** for the stated isolated Hamiltonian ensemble. It is not an exact statement about an arbitrary driven nonequilibrium snapshot; ergodic/mixing assumptions are required if long-time deterministic sampling is identified with this measure.

## 6. Canonical fixed-length ensemble

If the represented layer system is in thermal equilibrium at temperature $T$ while total normalized length $L$ is fixed, the configurational partition function is

$$
\boxed{
Z_M(L,\chi)
=
\int_{\lambda_i>0}
\exp\!\left[-\chi\sum_{i=1}^M\phi(\lambda_i)\right]
\delta\!\left(\sum_i\lambda_i-L\right)
d^M\lambda.
}
$$

The momenta factor out of the spacing marginal. By isolating one spacing, the exact finite-$M$ marginal is

$$
\boxed{
P_M(\lambda\mid L,\chi)
=
\frac{
 e^{-\chi\phi(\lambda)}
 Z_{M-1}(L-\lambda,\chi)
}{Z_M(L,\chi)},
\qquad
0<\lambda<L.
}
$$

This is already a nontrivial functional form for $P$ derived from the physical Hamiltonian and a fixed-length thermal ensemble. It uses the full LJ potential on its whole domain.

Define the dimensionless thermodynamic tensile force conjugate to $L$ by

$$
\boxed{
f_{\rm th}(L,\chi)
=-\frac1\chi\,\partial_L\ln Z_M(L,\chi).
}
$$

If the remaining $M-1$ spacings form a large reservoir and the removal of one spacing permits a local first-order entropy/free-energy expansion,

$$
\ln Z_{M-1}(L-\lambda,\chi)
\approx
\text{const}+\chi f_{\rm th}\lambda.
$$

Then the one-spacing marginal becomes the **CONTROLLED LARGE-RESERVOIR APPROXIMATION**

$$
\boxed{
P(\lambda)
\propto
\exp\{-\chi[\phi(\lambda)-f_{\rm th}\lambda]\}.
}
$$

The coefficients now have direct physical meanings: $\chi=EA_0a_0/(k_BT)$ and $f_{\rm th}$ is the force conjugate to total length. They are not arbitrary histogram-fit parameters.

## 7. Why a global tensile force-controlled Gibbs density does not exist

A crucial stability fact follows from the cohesive tail

$$
\phi(\lambda)\rightarrow0
\qquad(\lambda\rightarrow\infty).
$$

For any tensile $f>0$,

$$
w_f(\lambda)=\phi(\lambda)-f\lambda
\rightarrow-\infty.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{
\int_0^\infty e^{-\chi w_f(\lambda)}d\lambda=\infty
\qquad(f>0).
}
$$

Therefore a full-domain canonical equilibrium density under constant tensile force is **not normalizable**. This is not a mathematical defect; it is the statistical-mechanical signature that a cohesive pair allowed to separate without a length constraint can lower its force-biased energy indefinitely by opening.

For compressive $f<0$, by contrast, the linear force term grows positively as $\lambda\to\infty$ while LJ repulsion diverges as $\lambda\to0^+$, so a globally confined force-biased equilibrium can exist.

## 8. Metastable intact-basin distribution under subcritical tension

For $0<f<f_c$, $w_f$ still contains a local intact minimum at $\lambda_s$ separated from opening by the unstable stationary point $\lambda_b$. Define the dimensionless barrier

$$
\boxed{
\Delta w(f)
=
w_f(\lambda_b)-w_f(\lambda_s)>0.
}
$$

As $f\to f_c^-$,

$$
\lambda_s,\lambda_b\to\lambda_c,
\qquad
\Delta w\to0.
$$

If intrabasin equilibration is fast compared with the loading variation and escape is rare on that equilibration time, a physically motivated **CONTROLLED METASTABLE / LOCAL-EQUILIBRIUM APPROXIMATION** is the Gibbs measure conditioned on the intact basin:

$$
\boxed{
P_{\rm ms}(\lambda\mid f,\chi)
=
\frac{
\exp\{-\chi[w_f(\lambda)-w_f(\lambda_s)]\}
}{
\int_0^{\lambda_b}
\exp\{-\chi[w_f(s)-w_f(\lambda_s)]\}ds
}
\mathbf 1_{0<\lambda<\lambda_b}.
}
$$

This gives a crack-proximal instantaneous tail

$$
\boxed{
Q_c^{\rm ms}(f,\chi)
=
\int_{\lambda_c}^{\lambda_b(f)}
P_{\rm ms}(\lambda\mid f,\chi)d\lambda.
}
$$

$Q_c^{\rm ms}$ is an instantaneous metastable population, **not** a cumulative crack probability.

The barrier $\Delta w$ alone does not determine an escape rate. A Kramers-type rate would additionally require a physically derived bath/friction/phonon coupling and time scale. No such prefactor is inserted here.

## 9. Relation to the exact transport shape law

Milestone 11 obtained

$$
P
=\frac{C}{\Theta}
\exp\left[
\int\frac{\bar a-D_tu}{\Theta}d\lambda
\right].
$$

The present equilibrium results explain one physical limit of that exact kinematic/moment identity. In a locally equilibrated state with appropriate equipartition-like velocity statistics and mechanical force balance, the transport fields reduce to a Gibbs-type force-biased potential. Outside local equilibrium, however, the exact transport fields $\Theta$, $D_tu$, and neighbor correlations retain information that no equilibrium Gibbs marginal can replace.

Therefore the physical picture is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{exact driven transport}
\supset
\text{local/metastable equilibrium Gibbs limit}
\supset
\text{zero-temperature delta limit}.
}
$$

The inclusions are regime statements, not universal identities.

These are **DIMENSIONLESS MODEL DIAGNOSTICS**, not measured aluminum activation barriers or fatigue-life predictions. Conversion to a thermal exponent requires the physically justified $E_0=EA_0a_0$.

## 11. Main conclusions

The physical theory provides stronger constraints on the form of $P$ than an arbitrary probability-family choice:

- **zero-temperature stable equilibrium:** $P$ is a delta measure;
- **isolated equilibrium:** $P$ is a microcanonical marginal with a kinetic density-of-states factor;
- **fixed-length thermal equilibrium:** $P_M=e^{-\chi\phi}Z_{M-1}/Z_M$ exactly;
- **large-reservoir/local equilibrium:** a force-biased exponential of the full nonlinear potential emerges;
- **constant tensile force:** the global Gibbs integral diverges, so only fixed-length or metastable intact-basin distributions are physically meaningful before opening;
- **driven fatigue:** none of these equilibrium forms is automatically the full answer. The exact transport hierarchy remains necessary whenever local equilibration fails.

The immediate physical bottleneck is now sharply defined: determine the representative layer area $A_0$ and establish whether the deterministic cyclic state is close enough to a microcanonical/local-equilibrium regime for one of these physically derived forms to apply.

barrier $\Delta w$만으로 escape rate는 정해지지 않는다. Kramers-type rate를 쓰려면 physically derived bath/friction/phonon coupling과 time scale이 추가로 필요하다. 여기서는 그런 prefactor를 넣지 않는다.

## 9. Exact transport shape law와의 관계

Milestone 11에서는

$$
P
=\frac{C}{\Theta}
\exp\left[
\int\frac{\bar a-D_tu}{\Theta}d\lambda
\right]
$$

를 얻었다.

이번 equilibrium 결과는 그 exact kinematic/moment identity의 한 물리적 limit를 설명한다. 적절한 locally equilibrated state에서 velocity statistics가 equipartition-like하고 mechanical force balance가 성립하면 transport field가 Gibbs-type force-biased potential로 축약될 수 있다. 반대로 local equilibrium에서 멀어지면 exact transport의 $\Theta$, $D_tu$, neighbor correlation 정보를 equilibrium Gibbs marginal이 대신할 수 없다.

따라서 physical picture는

$$
\boxed{
\text{exact driven transport}
\supset
\text{local/metastable equilibrium Gibbs limit}
\supset
\text{zero-temperature delta limit}
}
$$

이다. 이는 regime 관계이지 universal identity가 아니다.

## 11. 핵심 결론

물리이론은 arbitrary probability-family 선택보다 $P$ 함수형을 더 강하게 제한한다.

- **zero-temperature stable equilibrium:** $P$는 delta measure다.
- **isolated equilibrium:** $P$는 kinetic density-of-states factor를 포함한 microcanonical marginal이다.
- **fixed-length thermal equilibrium:** $P_M=e^{-\chi\phi}Z_{M-1}/Z_M$가 exact다.
- **large-reservoir/local equilibrium:** full nonlinear potential의 force-biased exponential이 나온다.
- **constant tensile force:** global Gibbs integral은 발산하므로 opening 전에는 fixed-length 또는 metastable intact-basin distribution만 물리적으로 의미가 있다.
- **driven fatigue:** 이 equilibrium form 중 어느 것도 자동으로 전체 답이 아니다. local equilibration이 깨지면 exact transport hierarchy가 계속 필요하다.

즉 immediate physical bottleneck은 명확하다. representative layer area $A_0$를 결정하고 deterministic cyclic state가 microcanonical/local-equilibrium regime에 충분히 가까운지 검증해야 한다.
