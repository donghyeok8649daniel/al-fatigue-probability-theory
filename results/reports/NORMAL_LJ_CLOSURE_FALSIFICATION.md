# Normal-LJ Closure Falsification Result

## Result

The two-moment large-$M$ distribution closure was compared directly with deterministic 1D layer-LJ spacing snapshots at the same measured mean stretch and mean configurational energy.

No histogram parameter was fitted.

The closure reproduces the variance closely in the two tested near-equilibrium states, but it does not reproduce the complete empirical distribution.

For the slower case at approximately $t=10T$:

$$
D_{\rm KS}\approx0.15888,
$$

$$
\gamma_{1,\rm sim}\approx1.06221,
\qquad
\gamma_{1,\rm closure}\approx0.04408.
$$

For the second pre-instability case at $t=2T$:

$$
D_{\rm KS}\approx0.14584.
$$

The empirical and closure critical tails are both effectively zero in these pre-instability states.

## Interpretation

The variance agreement is not sufficient evidence that the closure is correct because the layer-LJ energy is locally quadratic near equilibrium. Matching mean energy therefore nearly fixes the variance automatically.

The observed skewness and CDF discrepancy show that mean stretch plus mean configurational energy do not fully determine the finite driven spacing distribution in this test.

This does **not yet uniquely falsify** the large-$M$ ensemble closure itself because the deterministic calculation contains only 31 spacings and may retain coherent, non-ergodic spatial structure.

The next discriminating test is a represented-system-size sweep.

# 한국어 번역 — Normal-LJ Closure 반증시험 결과

## 결과

two-moment large-$M$ distribution closure를 동일한 measured mean stretch와 mean configurational energy를 갖는 deterministic 1D layer-LJ spacing snapshot과 직접 비교했다.

histogram parameter는 전혀 fitting하지 않았다.

시험한 두 near-equilibrium state에서 closure는 variance는 상당히 가깝게 재현했지만 전체 empirical distribution은 재현하지 못했다.

느린 case의 약 $t=10T$에서

$$
D_{\rm KS}\approx0.15888,
$$

$$
\gamma_{1,\rm sim}\approx1.06221,
\qquad
\gamma_{1,\rm closure}\approx0.04408
$$

이다.

두 번째 pre-instability case의 $t=2T$에서는

$$
D_{\rm KS}\approx0.14584
$$

이다.

두 pre-instability state에서 empirical tail과 closure critical tail은 모두 사실상 0이다.

## 해석

equilibrium 근처의 layer-LJ energy가 local quadratic이므로 variance 일치만으로 closure가 맞다고 볼 수 없다. mean energy를 맞추는 것 자체가 variance를 거의 결정한다.

관찰된 skewness와 CDF discrepancy는 이번 시험에서 mean stretch와 mean configurational energy만으로 finite driven spacing distribution 전체가 정해지지 않는다는 것을 보여준다.

하지만 deterministic calculation은 spacing이 31개뿐이고 coherent/non-ergodic spatial structure를 유지할 수 있으므로 이것만으로 large-$M$ ensemble closure 자체가 완전히 반증됐다고 할 수도 없다.

다음 판별시험은 represented system-size sweep이다.
