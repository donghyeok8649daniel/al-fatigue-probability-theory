# Normal-LJ Spatial Correlation Result

## Result

The dynamically matched 1D layer-LJ chains retain strong spatial ordering that is absent from the one-point spacing density.

The nearest-neighbor normalized correlation increases from

$$
\rho_1=0.933439
$$

at $M=31$ to

$$
\rho_1=0.991302
$$

at $M=255$.

A uniform random permutation of exactly the same spacing values has the exact expected nonzero-lag correlation

$$
\mathbb E_{\rm perm}[\rho_k]=-rac1{M-1},
$$

which tends to zero as $M$ grows.

More importantly, the deterministic correlation profiles nearly collapse when plotted against

$$
\eta=\frac{k}{M}.
$$

The first zero crossing changes from approximately $0.356M$ to $0.346M$ over the tested range, and the positive-correlation area normalized by $M$ approaches about $0.184$.

## Scientific meaning

This is evidence that the driven reduced chain contains an $O(M)$ coherent spatial state. A one-point $p_\lambda(\lambda,t)$ cannot encode that state because it is invariant to permutations of the spacing labels.

Therefore the mismatch of the two-moment one-point closure is no longer plausibly attributable only to small finite-$M$ sampling error.

The next physically motivated state is a neighboring-spacing joint density $P_2(\lambda,\lambda',t)$ or an equivalent exact pair-correlation description.

This is a reduced-model numerical result, not a quantitative aluminum fatigue-life prediction.

# 한국어 번역 — Normal-LJ 공간상관 결과

## 결과

Dynamically matched 1D layer-LJ chain은 one-point spacing density에 들어 있지 않은 강한 spatial ordering을 유지한다.

nearest-neighbor normalized correlation은 $M=31$에서

$$
\rho_1=0.933439
$$

이고 $M=255$에서

$$
\rho_1=0.991302
$$

까지 증가한다.

반면 정확히 같은 spacing 값들을 uniform random permutation하면 모든 nonzero lag에 대한 기대상관은 정확히

$$
\mathbb E_{\rm perm}[\rho_k]=-rac1{M-1}
$$

이고 $M$이 커질수록 0으로 간다.

더 중요한 것은 deterministic correlation profile을

$$
\eta=\frac{k}{M}
$$

에 대해 그리면 거의 같은 곡선으로 collapse한다는 점이다.

첫 zero crossing은 tested range에서 약 $0.356M$에서 $0.346M$로 접근하고, positive-correlation area를 $M$으로 나눈 값은 약 $0.184$로 접근한다.

## 과학적 의미

이는 driven reduced chain에 $O(M)$ 규모의 coherent spatial state가 존재한다는 증거다. one-point $p_\lambda(\lambda,t)$는 spacing label을 permutation해도 변하지 않으므로 이 상태를 표현할 수 없다.

따라서 two-moment one-point closure의 mismatch를 단순한 작은 finite-$M$ sampling error만으로 설명하기는 어려워졌다.

다음으로 물리적으로 자연스러운 상태는 neighboring-spacing joint density $P_2(\lambda,\lambda',t)$ 또는 동등한 exact pair-correlation description이다.

이는 reduced-model numerical result이며 정량적인 aluminum fatigue-life prediction은 아니다.
